# Dish Form

The dish form opens as a side sheet whenever you click **Add Dish** or an existing dish card. Use it to set everything about how a dish appears on the menu.

## Photo

- Click the photo area or drag-drop an image (PNG / JPG, up to 5 MB).
- Recommended: 16:9 aspect ratio, well-lit, no watermark.
- If no photo is uploaded, the dish background uses the chosen **colour**.

## Name & Description

- **Name** — short, on-menu name. Avoid prefixes like "Special:" — use the *Available* toggle to handle that.
- **Description** — one or two sentences. Mention key flavours, preparation, or pairings. This text is searchable, so include any keywords guests might use to find the dish.

## Price

- Enter in euros (e.g. `12.50`). The currency symbol is added automatically.
- Decimals are optional but recommended for accuracy.

## Category

Pick from Starters, Mains, Desserts, Drinks, or Sides. Determines which category tab the dish appears under in the public menu.

## Colour

The accent colour shown behind the photo and as the card background tint. Pick something that matches the dish's mood or your menu palette.

## Ingredients (the most important section)

This is where allergens and dietary flags actually come from.

1. Start typing in the **Add ingredient** field — matches from the Ingredient Library appear as you type.
2. Pick one to attach it to this dish.
3. Repeat for every ingredient.

The **Allergen summary** under the ingredient list updates live — it shows every allergen found in any ingredient on this dish. Dietary flags work the opposite way: a dish is only shown as Vegetarian if **all** of its ingredients are vegetarian, and only Vegan if **all** are vegan. One non-vegan ingredient removes the flag from the whole dish.

**Missing an ingredient?** Open the [Ingredient Library](#) (sidebar) first, add it there, then come back.

## Available toggle

- **On** — appears on the menu and can be ordered.
- **Off** — hidden from menu but kept in the database. Use for seasonal items or stock-outs that affect only this dish (for full-stock-out, mark the underlying ingredient unavailable instead).

## Saving

- Click **Save** at the bottom. The sheet closes and the dish appears (or updates) in the Dishes view.
- Required fields are marked with a red asterisk — saving with anything missing shows inline errors.

## Deleting a Dish

> Only visible when editing an existing dish.

- Click **Delete** at the bottom of the sheet.
- Confirm in the prompt. The dish is removed permanently.
- Historical orders that referenced this dish are preserved — only the dish record is deleted.

## Tips

- **Build the ingredient list before naming the dish.** Allergens then surface and you can confirm the dish matches its category (e.g. "Vegan Bowl" actually qualifies as vegan).
- **Use the description for keyword density.** Guests search for "spicy", "creamy", "gluten-free" — including them in description boosts findability.
- **Photos sell.** A well-shot photo doubles order rates for new dishes; budget time for it.
