# Reservations List

This view shows every booking for a single day. Use it as the floor manager's home base: track who is arriving, who is seated, who is late, and who has finished.

## Choosing the Day

The page title shows the currently selected date. Change it via the **date button** in the top-right.

- A small calendar pops out.
- Today is highlighted.
- Click any day to jump straight to its list.

The subtitle ("X reservations this day") updates as you flip dates.

## Searching

The search box filters reservations as you type. Matches against:
- Guest name
- Phone or email (when entered on the booking)

Clear the box to see the full day again.

## Status Tabs

The pills next to the search filter by reservation lifecycle. Counts on each tab tell you how many bookings sit in that state today.

- **All** — every reservation
- **Scheduled** — booked, not yet arrived
- **Arrived** — guests are seated
- **Late** — past their booked time, no show yet
- **Departed** — finished and left
- **Cancelled** — cancelled by guest or staff
- **No Show** — did not arrive

Tabs combine with search; together they let you answer questions like *"Which 8 PM bookings are still scheduled?"*.

## Time Slots

Reservations are grouped by 30-minute slot. The small grey time markers (e.g. `19:00`) act as section headers, making it easy to scan upcoming bookings at a glance.

## Working a Reservation

Click any row to open the **detail sheet** — see the help icon inside that sheet for the full workflow (mark arrived, check in, cancel, etc.).

To create a brand-new booking, click **New Reservation** in the page header.

## Common Workflows

| I want to... | How |
| --- | --- |
| See tonight's full booking list | Default view; just confirm the date is today |
| Find one specific guest | Type their name in the search box |
| See who is late right now | Click the **Late** tab |
| Plan next Saturday's staffing | Pick the date in the calendar |
| Verify the no-show rate | Click **No Show** to count |

## Tips

- **Mark statuses promptly.** Late guests should be flagged so floor managers can release the table.
- **Use *No Show* not *Cancel*** when guests fail to arrive — it keeps no-show stats accurate.
- **Don't double-book.** Search first if you suspect a duplicate booking before clicking New Reservation.
- **Hand over at shift change.** Click into Arrived → Departed to see what is still active.
