# New Maintenance Task

Opens when you click **Add Task**. Use it to file a new problem on the maintenance queue. Aim for tasks that any team member could pick up and act on without follow-up questions.

## Title

A short, action-shaped sentence describing the problem.

- **Good:** "Replace ceiling bulb table 12"
- **Bad:** "Light"
- **Bad:** "Bulb"

Include the **location** and **what to do**. Future you, scanning the queue, should understand it in two seconds.

## Description / Notes

Detail the title can't carry. Aim for the next person picking this up cold:

- **Location** — which room, table, fixture
- **Severity** — is service blocked? safe to leave til morning?
- **Parts needed** — model number, spec, supplier
- **What's already been tried** — saves duplicate effort

Photos are gold here — attach if your installation supports it.

## Status

Defaults to **Unassigned** — a brand-new task is waiting to be picked up.

You can change this to **In Progress** at creation only if you are logging a job you have already started (e.g. you noticed the problem and began fixing it straight away). Leave it as **Unassigned** if someone else needs to pick it up.

## Assignee

Pick a maintenance team member. Three options:

- **Specific person** — Use when you know who is best (skill match, location, or they reported the problem).
- **Yourself** — When you intend to do the job.
- **Unassigned** — Leave blank to let the team claim it via the *Unassigned* filter.

When in doubt, leave unassigned — that's the default behaviour the queue expects.

## Priority (if shown)

A flag for tasks that block service vs. cosmetic fixes. Don't over-use — if everything is high priority, nothing is.

## Saving

- Click **Create Task** to file the task.
- The task appears at the top of the list, marked **New**.
- Validation errors highlight in red below the field.

## Tips

- **Be specific in the title.** Hours of fishing through the queue start with vague titles.
- **Don't bury severity in notes.** If the dishwasher is dead and service starts in 30 minutes, write that loud and clear.
- **Don't pre-assign to busy people.** Check their *My Tasks* load first.
- **Take photos.** A photo of the broken thing beats a sentence describing it.
