# New Reservation

This form creates a new booking on the currently selected day. Fields are simple — but every one matters for the floor team and the kitchen.

## Guest Name

The name on the booking, used when greeting the guest. Try to match the spelling they give over the phone — guests appreciate it.

- Use the full first + last name when possible.
- For walk-ins, the surname alone is fine.

## Phone

Phone number for short-notice contact (running late, table change). Optional but **strongly recommended** for any same-day booking.

- Include country code for international guests (e.g. `+31 6 …`).
- The form validates a reasonable phone shape — adjust if you get errors.

## Email

Optional. Used for confirmation emails (if your installation has email turned on) and to identify repeat guests.

## Party Size

How many people the booking is for. Determines which tables can accommodate the booking.

- Enter the **actual head count** — children included.
- Minimum party size is **2**. For a solo diner, enter 2 and note it in the Notes field.

## Date & Time

Date inherits from the currently selected day. Adjust the **time** to the slot the guest asked for.

- Use 24-hour format (e.g. `19:30`).
- Slots are typically 30 minutes apart; align bookings to slot starts when possible.

## Table

Pick from the dropdown of tables that match the party size. Tables already booked at this time are filtered out automatically.

- Leave on **"Auto-assign"** to let the system pick the best available table.
- Manually picking is useful for VIPs, accessibility needs, or window-seat requests.

## Notes

Free-form text for anything the kitchen or floor team needs to know:

- **Allergies** — even if you've ticked them elsewhere, restating in notes helps the kitchen.
- **Special requests** — high chair, anniversary, wheelchair access.
- **Source** — phone, walk-in, partner hotel referral.

The kitchen reads these — write them as if you were briefing the head chef.

## Saving

- Click **Create Reservation** to save.
- The booking appears in the list under its time slot.
- Validation errors highlight in red below the offending field.

## Tips

- **Take the phone number** for any booking less than 24 hours away — same-day cancellations and table swaps are common.
- **Don't double-book.** The table dropdown filters availability, but conflicts on overlapping bookings can still happen — verify time bounds.
- **Use notes for the kitchen.** "Severe nut allergy" in the notes is more visible than a flag they might miss.
- **For groups > 8,** check with the kitchen before confirming. Tables that big often need menu pre-orders.
