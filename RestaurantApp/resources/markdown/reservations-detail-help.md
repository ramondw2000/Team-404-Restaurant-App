# Reservation Detail

This panel opens when you click any reservation row. It is the working surface for one booking — checking in, marking statuses, editing, and cancelling.

## Header

Shows the guest's initials, full name, and booking date + time. Use it to confirm you have the right booking before changing anything.

## Status Stepper

A visual timeline of reservation states. Click a step to advance the booking to that stage.

| Step | Meaning | When to click |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | Booked, awaiting arrival | (Default — set on creation) |
| **Arrived** | Guests are seated | When the host walks them to the table |
| **Departed** | They have left | When the table is cleared |

### Late & No Show
For bookings that don't go to plan:

- **Late** — Set automatically if the guest hasn't arrived past their slot. You can also mark it manually.
- **No Show** — Set manually when you give up waiting (typically after 15–20 minutes).
- **Cancelled** — Set when the guest cancels (in advance or on arrival).

Status changes are immediate and visible on the list view's status tabs.

## Details Grid

The body grid shows everything captured on creation:

- Guest name, phone, email
- Party size
- Date & time
- Assigned table
- Notes (allergens, special requests)

Verify these before greeting the guest — especially allergens.

## Actions

### Edit
Opens the booking in edit mode. Change time, party size, table, or notes. Useful when:
- A guest calls to amend party size
- You need to move them to a different table on arrival
- You're adding a late-discovered allergy

### Cancel
Marks the reservation as Cancelled and frees the table. Confirms before applying — cancellation is reversible by re-editing, but the slot may be re-booked in between.

### Delete
Removes the reservation entirely (where allowed). Prefer **Cancel** unless the booking was created in error — Cancel preserves the history.

## Closing the Panel

- Press **Escape**
- Click outside the panel
- Click the close button (if shown)

## Workflows

### Guest arrives
1. Open their booking from the list.
2. Mark **Arrived**.
3. Walk them to the table shown in the details grid.

### Guest is late
1. Find the booking via the **Late** tab (or it auto-promotes).
2. Try to call the phone number in the details.
3. After your cut-off, click **No Show** to free the table.

### Guest wants to amend on arrival
1. Open their booking.
2. Click **Edit** in actions.
3. Update party size or table, save.
4. Mark **Arrived**.

## Tips

- **Always verify the name before marking Arrived.** Two same-time bookings can confuse hosts.
- **Move Late to No Show only after trying the phone.** Genuine late arrivals are common.
- **Note allergens in big bold** — kitchen reads the notes, not your memory.
- **Status changes are immediate.** Don't pre-emptively mark Departed just because a guest stood up — they may be returning from the loo.
