# Add a Drink to the Order

This modal opens whenever you click the **+** on a drink card. Use it to set the quantity and add any prep notes the bartender needs.

## Drink Name & Price

The header shows the drink and unit price as a confirmation. If this isn't what the guest asked for, click the **X** and pick the right card.

## Quantity

Two buttons next to a number control the quantity.

- **+** increases by one
- **−** decreases by one (minimum is 1)
- The number in the middle is the live total

If a guest wants four of the same drink, hit **+** three times. Faster than re-opening the card.

## Notes for the bar

Free-form text for any modifier the bartender needs to know:

- "Extra ice"
- "No straw"
- "Lots of lemon"
- "Make it strong"
- "Allergy: nuts — no garnish"

These print on the bartender's ticket. Keep them short and imperative — the bartender scans, not reads.

### Allergen notes
If a guest mentions an allergy, write it here even if the dish is marked free-from that allergen. Bartenders cross-check.

## Add to Order

- Click **Add to Order** to confirm and close the modal.
- The drink with this quantity + notes joins the cart at the bottom of the screen.
- The card's quantity badge updates immediately.

## Cancel

- Click **Cancel** or press **Escape** to close without adding.
- Useful if the guest changes their mind mid-order.

## Tips

- **Set quantity first, notes second** — quantity changes are common, notes rarely.
- **Don't use notes for "and a side of X"** — that's a separate cart item; close this modal and click + on the side.
- **Notes are per-drink, not per-order** — five of the same drink with five different notes need five separate cart entries (click + five times).
- **Spell out the modifier** — "no ice" is clearer than "naked".
