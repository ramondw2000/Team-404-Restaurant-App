## Getting Started

### Logging In
1. Go to the login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter your password
4. Click "Log in"

**Forgot your password?**
- Click "Forgot password?" on the login page
- Enter your email
- Check your inbox for a reset link
- Follow the instructions to set a new password

### First Time Logging In?
Your manager should have given you your login credentials. If you haven't received them, please ask your supervisor.

## Your Dashboard

After logging in, you'll see your personal dashboard. This is your home base.

**What you'll see:**
- A welcome message with your name
- Large buttons for each area you have access to
- Only the sections you need will be shown (based on your role)

**To go anywhere in the system:**
Simply click the big card for the section you want. For example, click "Kitchen Orders" to see what meals need to be prepared.

## Taking Orders

This is where you create orders for guests at tables.

### Starting a New Order
1. Go to **Table Management**
2. Find the table where your guests are sitting
3. Click on the table
4. Click "Create Order" (or "Resume Order" if they already have one)

### Adding Items to an Order
1. You'll see the menu on the left, categories in the middle
2. Click a category (like "Starters" or "Mains")
3. Click the dish you want to add
4. Set the quantity (how many)
5. Add any special notes (like "no onions" or "extra sauce")
6. Click "Add to Order"

**Tips:**
- Use the search bar at the top to quickly find dishes
- Use the filters to show only vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free options
- You can see dietary info and allergens for each dish

### Sending the Order to Kitchen/Bar
When everything is added:
1. Review the order summary on the right
2. Click "Submit Order"
3. The kitchen and/or bar will automatically receive the order

### Adding More Items Later
If guests want to order more:
1. Go back to the table
2. Click "Resume Order"
3. Add more items
4. Submit again—only the new items will be sent

## Managing Tables

This section shows you the layout of the restaurant.

### Viewing Tables
- You'll see a map of your restaurant floor plan
- Each table shows its current status:
  - **Green/White**: Table is free
  - **Red**: Table is occupied
  - **Yellow**: Table is reserved
  - **Orange**: Table needs cleaning
  - **Gray**: Table is out of order

### What You Can Do
- **See table details**: Click any table to see who's there, their order status, etc.
- **Create orders**: Click a table and then "Create Order"
- **Resume orders**: If a table already has an active order, you'll see a "Resume Order" button

### For Managers & Maintenance: Editing Floor Plans
If you need to change the restaurant layout:
1. Click "Edit Mode" (top right)
2. You can now:
   - Drag tables to move them
   - Resize tables
   - Add new tables from the sidebar
   - Delete tables
   - Copy and paste tables
3. Changes aren't saved until you click "Save Changes"
4. You can discard changes if you make a mistake

**Tip:** Tables snap to a grid to help you align them nicely. You can turn this off if needed.

## Kitchen & Bar Orders

These screens show all orders that need to be prepared.

### Kitchen Orders (For Chefs & Kitchen Staff)

**What you'll see:**
- A list of all pending food orders
- Each order shows the table number, time ordered, and what dishes are needed
- Each dish shows any special notes from the guest

**Marking Items Ready:**
When a dish is cooked and ready to go out:
1. Find the dish in the list
2. Click "Mark Ready"
3. The server will know they can pick it up

**Completing an Order:**
When all items for a table are served:
1. Click "Complete Order" 
2. The order moves to "Completed Orders"

### Bar Orders (For Bar Staff)

**What you'll see:**
- Two types of orders:
  - **Table orders**: Drinks for guests at tables
  - **Bar orders**: Drinks for guests at the bar

**Managing Drinks:**
- You'll see drink orders with any special requests
- Mark drinks as "Served" when you deliver them
- Pending drinks stay highlighted so you know what to make next

## Dishes & Menu

This is where the menu is managed (mainly for managers and chefs).

### Browsing the Menu
- See all dishes with photos
- Click categories to filter (Starters, Mains, Desserts, etc.)
- Use the sidebar to switch between different menus

### Adding a New Dish (Chefs & Managers)
1. Click "Add Dish" (usually a plus button or at the top)
2. Fill in:
   - **Name**: What the dish is called
   - **Price**: How much it costs
   - **Description**: What's in it
   - **Dietary tags**: Check boxes for vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal, etc.
   - **Allergens**: Select any allergens present (nuts, dairy, shellfish, etc.)
   - **Photo**: Upload a nice picture of the dish
3. Click "Save"

### Editing a Dish
1. Find the dish in the grid
2. Click on it
3. Make your changes
4. Click "Save"

### Managing Menus (Managers)
- **Create a menu**: Give it a name (like "Summer Menu 2025")
- **Add categories**: Like "Starters," "Pasta," "Grill"
- **Add dishes to categories**: Drag dishes or use the add button
- **Publish/Unpublish**: Only published menus appear when taking orders

### Ingredient Library
This is a list of all ingredients used in the kitchen. It helps with managing what goes into each dish.

## Reservations

For receptionists and managers handling guest bookings.

### Viewing Reservations
- See a list of all upcoming bookings
- Shows guest name, time, party size, and status

### Making a New Reservation
1. Click "New Reservation"
2. Enter:
   - Guest name
   - Phone number
   - Date and time
   - Number of people
   - Any special requests
3. Click "Save"

### Updating a Reservation
- **Change details**: Click the reservation, edit the info, save
- **Change status**: 
  - "Confirmed" - Booking is set
  - "Seated" - Guests have arrived
  - "No-show" - Guests didn't arrive
  - "Cancelled" - Booking cancelled

## Statistics & Reports

For managers to see how the restaurant is performing.

### What You Can See
- **Total Sales**: How much money was made
- **Number of Orders**: How many orders were taken

### Viewing Recent Orders
Scroll down to see the most recent completed orders with all the details.

## Managing Staff (Managers Only)

This section is for restaurant managers to handle staff accounts.

### Viewing Staff Accounts
- See a list of everyone who can log in
- Filter by role (chefs, servers, receptionists, etc.)
- See how many people are in each role

### Adding a New Staff Member
1. Click "Add Account"
2. Enter:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Password (or let the system generate one)
3. Assign roles (what they can do):
   - **Server**: Can take orders, view tables
   - **Chef**: Can view kitchen orders, mark items ready
   - **Bar Staff**: Can view bar orders
   - **Receptionist**: Can manage reservations, view tables
   - **Manager**: Can do everything
4. Click "Create"

### Editing a Staff Member
1. Find the person in the list
2. Click "Edit"
3. Change their details or roles
4. Save

### Deleting an Account
1. Find the person
2. Click "Delete"
3. Confirm (this can't be undone!)

### Managing Roles
**What are roles?**
Roles determine what someone can do in the system. For example, a "Chef" role lets someone view kitchen orders.

**Creating a Custom Role:**
1. Go to the "Roles" tab
2. Click "Create Role"
3. Give it a name and pick a color
4. Choose what permissions it has (what the person can see/do)
5. Save

**Note:** Some roles are built-in and can't be deleted.

### User Impersonation
Sometimes you need to see what another user sees. Instead of asking for their password:
1. Find their account
2. Click "Impersonate"
3. The system logs you in as them
4. Do what you need to do
5. Click "Stop Impersonating" to return to your account

**Important:** Only use this for troubleshooting or training!

## Tips & Troubleshooting

### General Tips
- **Can't find something?** Use the search bar—it's at the top of most pages
- **Made a mistake?** Most things can be edited or undone. Look for "Edit" or "Cancel" buttons
- **System seems slow?** Try refreshing the page (press F5 or Ctrl+R)
- **Lost your place?** Click "Dashboard" in the navigation to go back home

### Common Issues

**"I can't access this feature"**
- Your account might not have permission
- Ask your manager to check your role settings

**"My order disappeared"**
- Check the "Completed Orders" section
- Orders move there when marked complete

**"I accidentally deleted something"**
- Contact your manager immediately
- Some things can be recovered, some can't

**"The kitchen didn't get my order"**
- Make sure you clicked "Submit Order" (not just added items)
- Check that the order is showing in the Kitchen Orders screen

### Keyboard Shortcuts
- **Escape**: Close pop-ups and side panels
- **Enter**: Confirm buttons (when a dialog is open)
- **Tab**: Move between form fields
